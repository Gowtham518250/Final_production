Retail Mind Privacy Policy

Retail Mind is a point-of-sale app for shop owners (Android package com.retailmind.app). This policy covers the mobile app and the Retail Mind cloud services used to sync shop data. Last updated 7 November 2026.

Why we read SMS

SMS permission can expose SMS message content to Retail Mind. The app uses SMS access only to look for incoming payment-related messages from supported banks and payment services. Inbox threads are not displayed. Unrelated messages may be seen briefly while filtering and are not stored as shop records. Manual billing remains available without SMS access.

Why we use notification access

Notification access can expose notification text from apps on the device. Retail Mind looks for supported payment alerts. Unrelated notifications may be seen before they are filtered. A notification is not proof of settlement.

Why we use Accessibility

Accessibility is limited to reading on-screen text from bank and UPI apps so a payment confirmation can be matched to a bill. Retail Mind does not tap, swipe, type, or control other apps. Gesture control is disabled. Manual billing remains available if this is off.

Data we process

Shop catalog, sales, inventory, staff attendance, and account details you enter. Optional payment-alert fields (amount, reference, source app or SMS sender). Device identifiers used to keep your session signed in. Google Firebase may process crash, auth, and messaging data for the Android app.

Retention and rights

Business records stay until you delete them or delete the account. You can export data from Settings → Export Data and delete the account from Settings → Delete Account.
